Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the Doc Editor app (package com.pixel.sage.docx), published by PixelSageNest, handles your information.

Your documents

Documents are opened, edited and converted on your device. The app asks for access to your device's storage so it can find and save your files. We do not upload your documents unless you choose to share a file, in which case that file is sent to our server to create a share link.

Scanning

Scan to PDF uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, provided by Google Play services. The app only receives the pages you choose to keep.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID and usage data to show and measure ads. Where required, we ask for your consent first. See how Google uses data from apps.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. This includes anonymous usage events, device information and crash logs. It does not include the contents of your documents.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from the app's Profile screen, or email us to ask for your data to be deleted. Uninstalling the app removes the data it stores on your device.

Contact

Questions about this policy: pixelsagenest@gmail.com
